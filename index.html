ABOUT THE HACKATHON

WHAT IS MANTHAN 2.0?

MANTHAN 2.0 is an AI-focused hackathon designed to bring together students, innovators, problem-solvers, and future leaders to create intelligent solutions for real-world challenges.

The 2026 Edition: Architecting Intelligent Futures

The 2026 edition, themed “Architecting Intelligent Futures,” focuses on using Artificial Intelligence to address challenges across diverse sectors while keeping innovation, responsibility, and societal impact at the centre.

Rather than simply utilizing pre-packaged AI tools, MANTHAN 2.0 pushes teams to architect end-to-end intelligent systems that are ethical, scalable, and capable of creating meaningful long-term transformation.

Real-World Problem Solving

Work on high-impact challenges requiring practical, AI-driven solutions.

AI-First Innovation

Collaborative Ideation

Unite multidisciplinary talent to turn abstract problems into structured blueprints.

Idea to Execution

CORE FOUNDATIONS

THE FOUR PILLARS OF MANTHAN 2.0

The hackathon is built around four core principles that guide participants from initial ideation to final execution.

01

Responsible AI

  • Design AI solutions with ethics and responsibility at their core.
  • Consider fairness, transparency, privacy, and responsible use of technology.
  • Focus on solutions that create value without compromising trust.
02

AI Governance

  • Think about how AI systems should be designed, managed, and monitored.
  • Consider accountability, auditing, and responsible decision-making.
  • Build solutions that operate within appropriate ethical & legal frameworks.
03

Sustainability

  • Create solutions that consider long-term environmental and social consequences.
  • Explore how technology can contribute to sustainable development goals.
  • Prioritize ideas that continue creating lasting value beyond the hackathon.
04

Societal Impact

  • Focus on real problems that affect communities and everyday lives.
  • Measure success not only through technology, but through meaningful outcomes.
  • Build solutions that have the potential to create positive, lasting change.
EVENT SCHEDULE & ROUNDS

THE MANTHAN JOURNEY

Three intense stages designed to take your idea from concept blueprint to working MVP and an adrenaline-pumping live defense.

DAY 1 | ROUND 01

DISCOVER — AI Blueprint & Ideation Sprint

The first day begins with teams identifying a challenge and transforming it into a structured AI solution blueprint. Participants move from understanding the challenge to formulating an initial concept that can eventually become a functional solution.

WHAT'S INCLUDED:

1
Opening Ceremony & Theme Briefing

Begin with an introduction to the theme, challenge environment, and expectations.

2
Challenge Statement Selection

Select your challenge track and clearly define the problem your team will address.

3
Ideation Sprint

Brainstorm, discuss, and develop high-potential AI approaches to the selected problem.

4
AI Blueprint Submission

Convert your idea into a structured blueprint explaining your proposed AI architecture.

5
Mentor Feedback & Review

Receive directional feedback from industry mentors to strengthen your concept.

ROUND 01 OUTCOME

All participating teams advance to the Prototype Development Round (Day 2), where ideas move from concept toward execution.

No Elimination on Day 1 100% Teams Advance
DAY 2 | ROUND 02 (ELIMINATION)

DEVELOP — Prototype Development & Evaluation Sprint

Day 2 focuses on turning the blueprints developed during the first round into a functional AI prototype. Teams work under time constraints while receiving deep technical guidance and feedback from mentors.

WHAT'S INCLUDED:

1
MVP / Prototype Development

Build a minimum viable product or working software prototype based on your Day 1 blueprint.

2
Technical Mentorship Sessions

Interact with technical mentors for code debugging, architecture reviews, and AI model tuning.

3
Mid-Round Checkpoints

Review team progress, identify bottlenecks, and make improvements before the final evaluation.

4
Prototype Demonstration

Demonstrate how your solution works and communicate its key features and real-world value.

5
Jury Evaluation & Pitch

Present your prototype to the jury, demonstrating feasibility, strength, and societal impact.

ELIMINATION ROUND

Teams present their prototypes to the expert jury. Only the highest-performing teams will be shortlisted for the Grand Finale!

Jury Cutoff Shortlisted Finalists Advance
DAY 3 | ROUND 03 • THE GRAND FINALE

DEFEND — The Live Charter Challenge

THINK • BUILD • DEFEND. The final round puts shortlisted teams to the ultimate test. Finalists receive a surprise AI challenge on the spot and must rapidly develop a solution within a limited time under intense pressure.

WHAT'S INCLUDED:

1
Surprise Problem Statement Reveal

Receive a completely new, unannounced challenge at the beginning of the final round.

2
Live Innovation Sprint

Work against the clock to analyze the sudden problem and synthesize an innovative approach.

3
Rapid Prototype / Solution Design

Quickly engineer a functional concept or prototype directly addressing the surprise challenge.

4
Grand Jury Presentation

Present your high-speed solution directly before the Grand Jury and audience.

5
Q&A and Strategic Defense

Answer rigorous jury questions, defend your technical decisions, and justify architectural choices.

CROWNING THE CHAMPIONS

The champions of MANTHAN 2.0 are evaluated on:

  • Innovation
  • Execution & Technical Rigor
  • Critical Thinking Under Pressure
  • Persuasive Defense & Presentation
